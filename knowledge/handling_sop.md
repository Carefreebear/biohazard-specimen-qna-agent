# Specimen Transportation SOP — Synthetic Training Template

This document is demonstration content. Replace it with the current organization-approved SOP before operational use.

## Pre-pickup verification

Confirm the pickup identity, destination, requested service level, and package count. Inspect the exterior without opening the package. Accept only containers that are closed, dry, labeled according to the approved procedure, and placed in the required secondary and outer packaging. Record exceptions before departure.

## Chain of custody

Record each transfer with the package identifier, date, time, origin, destination, and the names or approved identifiers of both parties. Keep custody records with the shipment record. Do not include unnecessary patient information in dispatch notes.

## Temperature control

Use the validated transport container and conditioning method specified by the shipment profile. Record the container ID and required temperature band. Keep the container closed during transport and deliver it directly to the authorized receiving location. Report a temperature alarm or unknown temperature condition as an exception.

## Vehicle handling

Secure the closed outer package upright in the designated cargo area so it cannot slide, tip, or contact food or personal items. Maintain separation from unrelated cargo. Do not open, repackage, or directly handle the specimen during the route.

## Spill leak or damaged package

Do not touch, open, or continue transporting a leaking, visibly contaminated, broken, or damaged package. Isolate the area, keep others away, and contact the supervisor or designated safety lead. Follow the organization spill-response and incident-reporting procedures. Seek emergency assistance when directed by the approved response plan.

## Delivery exception

Release the shipment only to an authorized receiver. If the site is closed, the receiver cannot be verified, or delivery would exceed the approved time or temperature limits, keep the package secured and contact dispatch for instructions. Document the exception and final disposition.

## Exposure response

For a splash, puncture, direct contact, or suspected exposure, stop work and follow the organization exposure-control plan immediately. Notify the supervisor or safety lead and obtain medical evaluation according to the approved procedure. Complete the required incident record without adding patient identifiers that are not needed.
